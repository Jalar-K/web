Drafts stay on this phone

Nothing leaves this page until you tap Send. If you close the tab, whatever you wrote will still be waiting here next time.

What this page remembers

  • When you open it, and what time it was where you are.
  • Your city and country, worked out roughly from your internet connection — never a street or an address.
  • Which games you played and how you did.
  • Messages you send, once you tap Send.
  • Your exact location only if you tap the button, and only until you tap Undo.

Anything you type and don't send stays on this phone. Kendrick never sees it.
